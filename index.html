Field Notes

Player-facing help only. Debug stays off the map.

  • Select units by clicking their tile.
  • Move to highlighted adjacent tiles.
  • Settlers can found cities and improve worked land.
  • Cities grow with food and build warriors or settlers with production.
  • Attack enemy units or cities by moving onto adjacent hostile tiles.
  • Use arrow keys to move the selected unit.
  • Press D to toggle map debug text.
  • Press Tab to cycle units, R to restart, and Enter to end turn.
